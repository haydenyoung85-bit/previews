Preventive care

Cleanings, regular checkups, dental sealants and patient education for children and adults in Irvine. Keeping a regular preventive schedule is one of the simplest ways to avoid bigger, costlier problems later, and it helps the whole family keep healthy smiles between visits to the Culver Plaza office.

02

Restorative dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, porcelain crowns, inlays and onlays, implants, and partial or complete dentures. Materials and techniques keep improving, and the doctors aim to offer the most current options available so damaged or missing teeth can be restored to look natural and work comfortably again.

03

Cosmetic dentistry

Porcelain veneers, Invisalign clear aligners, one-hour Boost in-office whitening and Opalescence take-home whitening for patients in Irvine and nearby communities. Whether it's straightening, brightening or reshaping, cosmetic care helps you keep your mouth healthy while improving how your smile looks.

Guards & sleep devices

Custom nightguards, sports mouthguards and anti-snoring or sleep apnea devices. A well-fitted guard protects teeth from grinding at night or impact on the field, and an oral appliance can be a comfortable option for patients and families dealing with snoring in Irvine.

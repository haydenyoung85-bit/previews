01

Aluminum patio covers

Solid, lattice and insulated aluminum patio covers for Roseville, Folsom and Granite Bay homes. Patio Pros builds with Duralum material, which has an embossed wood grain and baked-on enamel finish and is guaranteed for life against rust, rot, warping and twisting, so the cover stays good-looking with little upkeep.

02

Pergolas

Pergolas that add style and filtered shade to backyards in El Dorado Hills, Lincoln and the greater Sacramento area. A well-placed pergola defines an outdoor room for dining or relaxing, frames the view of your yard, and gives climbing plants a place to grow through the warm Valley summers.

03

Shade structures

Shade covers and canopies, including Shadetree Canopies, for patios, pools and outdoor living areas across the Sacramento Valley and foothills. With long, hot summers in Roseville and Folsom, the right shade structure makes your patio usable in the afternoon and protects furniture from harsh sun all season.

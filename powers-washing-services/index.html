Right method, right surface

Soft washing vs. pressure washing

They are not the same job. Using the wrong one is how roofs and siding get damaged.

Low pressure

Soft wash

Uses gentle water pressure and a cleaning solution that breaks down dirt, mildew and algae, then rinses away. It cleans deeply without blasting the surface.

  • Roofs
  • Vinyl siding and house exteriors
  • Surfaces that damage easily
High pressure

Pressure wash

Uses strong water pressure to strip heavy dirt and buildup off hard, durable surfaces. It is fast and effective where the material can take it.

  • Concrete and driveways
  • Sidewalks and patios
  • Hard, durable surfaces

Not sure which your property needs? We look at it, choose the method, and tell you why when we quote.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Usually not, as long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and have access to an outdoor water source. We will confirm the details with you when we quote the job.

Is soft washing safe for roofs?

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution, which is the right method for roofs and siding. High pressure can damage those surfaces, so we save it for hard surfaces like concrete.

What do you need from me?

The address, what you would like cleaned, and a good day to come out. Photos help if you have them. Tell us about anything delicate, such as plants, loose trim or areas that need extra care.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, school and institutional exteriors, and other commercial properties, as well as homes.

Questions

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Usually not, as long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and have access to an outdoor water source. We will confirm the details with you when we quote the job.

Is soft washing safe for roofs?

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution, which is the right method for roofs and siding. High pressure can damage those surfaces, so we save it for hard surfaces like concrete.

What do you need from me?

The address, what you would like cleaned, and a good day to come out. Photos help if you have them. Tell us about anything delicate, such as plants, loose trim or areas that need extra care.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, school and institutional exteriors, and other commercial properties, as well as homes.

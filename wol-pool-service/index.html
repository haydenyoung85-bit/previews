01

Weekly full service

Full service four times a month for Bakersfield homes: vacuuming as needed, brushing, testing water, keeping chemicals balanced, cleaning the pump basket and skimmer, checking filter pressure and cleaning the filter. You'll also be told about any problems spotted with the pool, before they turn into a costly repair.

02

Pump & filter repair

Repairs and installs for filters, pumps, lights, timers and panels, with free estimates. WOL installs sand filters and pumps from brands like Hayward, Sta-Rite, Waterway, Jandy and Pentair, so worn-out equipment in your Kern County backyard can be replaced and running again quickly.

Get a free estimate →
03

Commercial pools

Pool service for apartment complexes, hotels, condos, realtors and property management companies in Bakersfield and the southern San Joaquin Valley. Commercial pools see heavy use, and steady chemical balance and equipment care keep them clean, inviting and ready for residents and guests all season.

Request a bid →
04

Green pools & safety

Letting a pool turn green can be a code violation enforced by the city, and stagnant water breeds mosquitoes that can spread West Nile virus. Call WOL about getting a neglected Bakersfield pool back under control, and ask about pool alarms to help keep children safe around the water.
